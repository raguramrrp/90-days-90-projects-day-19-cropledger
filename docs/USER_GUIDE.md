# User Guide — CropLedger

1. **Overview (`/overview`):** Review daily reporting counts, highest/lowest modal prices, day-over-day price movers, followed watchlist crops, and 7D/30D/90D/1Y historical trajectories.
2. **Price Advisor (`/price-advisor`):** Choose a commodity, variety, origin market, quantity (quintals), transport mode (`₹/km` or fixed), commission (`%` or fixed), and handling costs. Markets are ranked by **Estimated Net Return**, and an explanation highlights when transport costs outweigh higher headline prices.
3. **Market Atlas (`/market-atlas`):** Explore verified Indian APMC coordinates with clustered price badges. Click any marker for min/max/modal ranges and freshness status. Markets without verified coordinates are listed transparently below the table.
4. **Crop Intelligence (`/crops`):** Switch between Daily, Weekly, Monthly, and Seasonal views, check volatility (CV%), and inspect arrival-volume vs price correlation.
5. **Market Comparison (`/market-comparison`):** Select up to 4 markets to compare headline modal price vs estimated net return side by side.
6. **Price Reports (`/reports`):** Filter by reporting date, state, district, market, category, commodity, or variety; sort any column; export filtered records to **CSV** or **PDF** or print directly.
7. **Forecast & Trends (`/forecast`):** Evaluate 7-day statistical projections (Moving Average, Lagged Baseline, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting) with strict chronological train/test holdout MAE and RMSE.
8. **Watchlist & Alerts (`/watchlist`):** Follow specific crop-market pairs and create threshold or percentage movement alerts evaluated in-app.
9. **Data Quality (`/data-quality`):** Inspect freshness classifications (`Recently updated`, `Delayed`, `Stale`, `Unavailable`), upload custom CSV files, sync `data.gov.in`, or clear demo data.
10. **Settings (`/settings`):** Save preferred state, district, crops, and default cost assumptions for the Price Advisor.
